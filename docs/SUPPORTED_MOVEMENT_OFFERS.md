# Supported suggestions and spoken completion

The live conversation receives a full-movement inventory derived from the connected, pre-torso renderer catalog. The current full demonstration is **wall angel**, including its supported wall-free arm illustration. Independent joint channels are not permission to invent an exercise.

For a broad stiffness/pain request, the agent clarifies the relevant location and movement-related pain as needed, offers only relevant registered choices, and waits for selection. When no supported movement fits, it explains the limitation. It must not diagnose, prescribe or promise relief. The live planner rejects unsupported exercise requests before calling its provider and validates that the generated movement belongs to the selected supported family.

Practice preserves the existing quiet recording flow. Only a real user completion cue or explicit review request authorizes `finish_practice`. Examples include “I’m done,” “I finished doing it,” “I’ve finished,” and “Please review that repetition.” Negated/future phrases such as “I’m not done,” “I haven’t finished,” and “When I’m done, what do I say?” cannot finish capture. A camera update cannot reuse an earlier demonstration or view request as the finish cue.
